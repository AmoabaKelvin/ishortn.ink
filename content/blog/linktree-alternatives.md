Linktree's free plan is good. You get unlimited links, a QR code and 28 days of analytics without paying. People look elsewhere for four reasons: Linktree doesn't support custom domains on any plan, clicks per link need the Starter plan ($9 a month, or $7 billed yearly), removing the Linktree footer needs Pro ($17 a month, or $14 billed yearly), and the free plan takes a 12% fee on tips and pay-to-unlock content.

The short version, if you already know what you want:

- **The cheapest page on your own domain:** Carrd Pro Standard, $19 a year.
- **Selling from your bio:** Beacons or Stan.
- **Unlimited links for free:** Linktree itself, Lnk.Bio or Milkshake.
- **A bio page plus short links and QR codes on your own domain, with clicks on every link:** iShortn Pro, $8 a month.

We make iShortn, one of the tools below. Every competitor fact comes from that company's own pricing page or help center, checked on 22 November 2026, in US dollars. Linktree and Milkshake show local prices in some countries, so check the live pages before you decide.

## When to stay on Linktree

Stay if you need lots of links and don't mind the footer, because the free plan is generous. Stay too if you sell digital products, courses or bookings and Pro's 0% Linktree fee works out cheaper than moving, or if you rely on its Link Apps, such as music pre-saves, video embeds and Instagram auto-replies. None of the cheaper tools below copies all of that.

## 1. iShortn: clicks on every link, and your own domain for $8

[iShortn](/pricing) is a link shortener with bio pages, custom domains, dynamic QR codes and click analytics. The bio page is one part of it, so you manage your bio links, the short links you post elsewhere and your QR codes in one account.

**Free:** 1 bio page at `ishortn.ink/p/yourname` with up to 3 link buttons, plus headings, text, an email button and social icons, which don't count toward the 3. The page shows views, unique visitors, clicks on each button and click-through rate, for the last 7 days. You also get 30 short links a month and 5 dynamic QR codes that don't expire. Views and clicks share an allowance of 1,000 tracked clicks a month. Past that, the page keeps working, but new visits aren't counted until the next month.

**Pro, $8 a month ($80 a year):** up to 3 bio pages with no limit on link buttons, on your own domain such as `links.yourshop.com`, with no iShortn badge, your own colours, fonts and button styles, and a custom social preview image. Pro includes 3 custom domains, 1,000 new links and 10,000 tracked clicks a month, a full year of detailed analytics, password-protected links and the API. On Pro, bio link buttons count toward the 1,000 links.

Compared with Linktree, iShortn shows clicks per link on the free plan, where Linktree starts that on Starter. Pro serves the page on your own domain, which no Linktree plan does, and removes the badge for $8 against Linktree Pro's $17.

**Where Linktree is better:** a free iShortn page holds 3 link buttons, and Linktree's holds unlimited links. iShortn has no store, tip jar, email sign-up form or video and music embeds. Linktree Pro shows referrer, location and device data for page visitors. Our bio page analytics show views and clicks per button, without that breakdown. Linktree also has a basic link shortener on every plan. The full side-by-side is on [iShortn vs Linktree](/compare/linktree).

## 2. Beacons: for creators who sell

[Beacons](https://beacons.ai/i/pricing) puts a link-in-bio page, a store, email marketing and a website builder in one product. Free includes unlimited links, traffic analytics, 50 email sends a month and unlimited digital products, with a 9% Beacons seller fee on store sales and affiliate commissions. Creator, at $10 a month ($8.33 billed yearly), adds a custom domain, which the help center says is "Free for your first year", and 500 email sends. The seller fee drops to 0% on Creator Plus, at $30 a month ($25 billed yearly), which is also the plan that removes Beacons branding.

**Where Beacons beats iShortn:** selling, email and DM automation. If your bio exists to sell, it does far more than we do. **Where iShortn is better:** if you don't sell, you don't pay for a store you won't use. Removing the branding costs $8 a month on iShortn, against $25 to $30 on Beacons. See [iShortn vs Beacons](/compare/beacons).

## 3. Carrd: the cheapest page on your own domain

[Carrd](https://carrd.co/pro) builds simple one-page websites, and many people use one as a link-in-bio page. Free gives you up to 3 sites on carrd.co addresses with Carrd branding. Pro Lite, at $9 a year, removes the branding. Pro Standard, at $19 a year, adds custom domains, forms, embeds and analytics for up to 10 sites. Pro Plus, at $49 a year, raises that to 25 sites. Carrd offers a 7-day free trial of Pro.

**Where Carrd beats iShortn:** price and design freedom. $19 a year for a page on your own domain costs less than three months of iShortn Pro, and you can add forms and embeds and lay the page out however you like. **Where iShortn is better:** Carrd is a site builder, so it has no short links for the posts, emails and flyers you share outside the page. If you want tracked short links and dynamic QR codes next to your bio page, iShortn has them in one account. See [iShortn vs Carrd](/compare/carrd).

## 4. Stan: a storefront, priced like one

[Stan](https://stan.store) is a store for creators who sell courses, coaching, memberships and digital downloads from their bio. There's no free plan. Creator costs $29 a month ($300 a year), Creator Pro $99 a month, and Creator Max $299 a month. Every plan has a 14-day free trial, and Stan says it charges "0% transaction fees on every plan", though payment processing fees still apply. A personal domain is listed only on Creator Max.

**Where Stan beats iShortn:** it's built for selling, from checkout pages to courses. **Where iShortn is better:** price, if you only need links. $29 a month is a lot for a page of links.

## 5. Lnk.Bio: unlimited links and one-time prices

[Lnk.Bio](https://lnk.bio) has a free plan with unlimited links, basic statistics, a QR code and 3 pages. Mini, at $0.99 a month, adds scheduling, password-protected links and advanced analytics. Unique is a one-time payment of $24.99 and removes the Lnk.Bio logo. Connecting a domain you already own is on Ultra, at $89.99 a year.

**Where Lnk.Bio beats iShortn:** unlimited links for free, and a one-time price to remove branding. **Where iShortn is better:** a domain you already own costs $80 a year on iShortn Pro (paid yearly) against $89.99 on Lnk.Bio Ultra, and Pro also includes short links, dynamic QR codes and up to 3 domains.

## 6. Milkshake: a mini website built on your phone

[Milkshake](https://milkshake.app) is a phone app that builds a small website from swipeable cards. Free includes unlimited links and cards and 30 days of insights. Lite, at $2.99 a month, removes Milkshake branding. Pro is $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year, with 365 days of insights. Connecting your own domain needs Pro+, at $99.99 a year or $29.99 every 3 months. Milkshake notes that prices may vary by country and platform.

**Where Milkshake beats iShortn:** unlimited links for free, and branding removal from $2.99 a month. **Where iShortn is better:** for your own domain, iShortn Pro costs $80 a year paid yearly against $99.99 for Pro+, and adds short links and dynamic QR codes.

## 7. Taplink: pages with forms and payments

[Taplink](https://taplink.at/en/pricing/) has a free Basic plan with unlimited links and page view statistics. Pro, at $48 a year on yearly billing, adds clicks analytics and media blocks. Business, at $96 a year, adds a custom domain, removes Taplink branding and includes forms, payments and a simple CRM.

**Where Taplink beats iShortn:** lead forms and payments on the page. **Where iShortn is better:** clicks per link on the free plan (Taplink's free plan counts page views), and short links and QR codes in the same account.

## How to move off Linktree without losing visitors

1. **Build the new page first.** Add your links, check each one on your phone, and publish.
2. **Change your bios.** Replace the linktr.ee address in your Instagram, TikTok and YouTube bios, your email signature and your Google Business Profile.
3. **Keep the Linktree page live.** Old posts, printed flyers and Linktree's own QR code still point at it. Edit it down to one link to your new page. On Starter and up, Linktree's Redirect link sends visitors straight on to a link you choose.
4. **Use your own domain this time.** If your new page lives on `links.yourbrand.com`, the next move only means pointing the domain somewhere else. Every printed link and QR code keeps working. If you print QR codes, a dynamic code from our [free QR code generator](/qr-code-generator) can point at your page.

## Quick answers

**Is there a free Linktree alternative?** Yes. Lnk.Bio, Milkshake, Taplink, Beacons, Carrd and iShortn all have free plans. Lnk.Bio, Milkshake, Taplink and Beacons allow unlimited links on theirs. iShortn's free page holds 3 link buttons.

**Which Linktree alternative lets me use my own domain?** All of the tools above except Linktree, on a paid plan: Carrd from $19 a year, iShortn from $8 a month, Beacons from $10 a month, Taplink at $96 a year, Lnk.Bio at $89.99 a year, Milkshake at $99.99 a year and Stan on Creator Max.

**Does Linktree show clicks per link on the free plan?** No. Linktree's pricing page lists individual link insights from Starter.

**Can I import my Linktree links into iShortn?** No, there's no import, so you add the links by hand. With a handful of links that takes a few minutes.
