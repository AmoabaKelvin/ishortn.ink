TinyURL has been around for more than 20 years, and it still does the basic job well. You paste a long link, you get a short one, and it never expires. Most people go looking for an alternative after they share a link and want to know whether anyone clicked it. TinyURL's free plan can't tell you.

This guide covers what TinyURL Free does and doesn't include, then four alternatives, what each one gives you for free, and what it costs when you need more. We make iShortn, one of the options. Every competitor fact comes from that company's own pricing or help pages, checked on 21 November 2026. Prices change, so check the live pages before you decide.

## What TinyURL Free includes, and what it doesn't

From [TinyURL's pricing page](https://tinyurl.com/app/pricing) and its post on [what the free plan includes](https://tinyurl.com/blog/what-included-tinyurl-free/):

Free gives you 30 links a month on `tinyurl.com`, custom aliases, a QR code for each link, a list of the links you've made, and links that never expire.

Free doesn't include:

- Any click analytics. The pricing table shows no analytics dashboard, no total clicks and no analytics history on Free.
- Changing where a link points after you've made it.
- Deleting links.
- Setting an expiry date.
- Connecting your own domain. The pricing page says users who downgrade from a paid plan can keep 1 domain active, but new free users can't bring one.

The missing analytics matters most if you're promoting anything. A link you can't measure is hard to justify in a campaign, a newsletter or a printed flyer. The no-editing rule matters for QR codes too. TinyURL's QR code points at a link you can't change, so if the page moves, the printed code goes to the old address.

TinyURL's paid plans start with Pro, which is $9 a month billed annually ($108 a year) or $11 month to month, for 125 links a month with unlimited tracked clicks, 2 years of analytics history and 3 branded domains. Extra links cost $1.50 per 10, and there are 250 and 500 link tiers. Bulk starts at $69 a month billed annually for 50,000 links and 50,000 tracked clicks, and Bulk links expire after 90 days by default.

## 2. Short.io: the most free clicks and a free custom domain

[Short.io's](https://short.io/pricing) free plan is generous where TinyURL's is thin. It gives you up to 5 custom domains, 50,000 tracked clicks a month and 1,000 branded links, for one user. Short.io's help center says you can change a link's destination on Free as often as you like.

**Watch for:** the 1,000 links are a total for the life of the account, not a monthly allowance. If you make 50 links a month, you'll run out in under two years. Free is for a single user.

**Best for:** anyone whose main reason for leaving TinyURL is wanting their own domain without paying.

## 3. Rebrandly: branded links with a small free plan

[Rebrandly](https://www.rebrandly.com/pricing) Free includes 10 links and 10 QR codes a month, unlimited clicks and scans, custom back-halves, one link gallery and 1 custom domain, which can be a domain you already own or a free `.bio` domain from Rebrandly. Detailed analytics covers 100 clicks a month. Free QR codes and the link gallery carry a Rebrandly watermark, and changing a link's destination starts on Essentials, with 10 edits a month.

**Watch for:** 100 clicks of detailed data runs out fast on anything you promote. 10 links a month is a third of TinyURL Free.

**Best for:** brands that expect to grow into Rebrandly's bigger plans and want to start there.

## 4. Bitly: the familiar name, but not an upgrade on Free

It has no click or scan analytics and no destination editing, and it shows an ad page before each link opens. Paid analytics starts on Core at $10 a month billed annually, and a custom domain starts on Growth at $29 a month.

## When TinyURL is still the right choice

TinyURL is fine if you don't need to know who clicked. If you share a long link once in a chat or an email, TinyURL is quick, its links never expire, and you can shorten 30 links a month on a free account. If you already pay for TinyURL Pro, it keeps 2 years of analytics history, so there's little reason to move.

Switch when you need to measure clicks, change links after sharing them, or print QR codes you might need to update.

## How to switch from TinyURL

TinyURL links on `tinyurl.com` stay on TinyURL's domain, so you can't move them. They'll keep working. Make new links on the new service and update the places you control, such as your bio, website, email signature and pinned posts.

If you're making the move anyway, consider putting new links on your own domain, like `go.yourbrand.com`, so you never have to switch short links again.

## FAQ

### Does TinyURL have analytics on the free plan?

No. TinyURL's pricing page shows no analytics on Free. Click tracking starts on Pro, at $9 a month billed annually.

### Can I edit a TinyURL link for free?

No. Changing a link's destination and deleting links are paid features on TinyURL.

### Do TinyURL links expire?

Free and Pro links never expire by default, according to TinyURL's pricing page. Bulk links expire after 90 days by default.
