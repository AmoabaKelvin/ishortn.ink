## What marketers need from a link shortener

### Your own domain on the links

A link on `go.yourbrand.com` tells people who sent it. That matters in emails, SMS and printed material, where a random string on a shortener's domain can look like spam. Our guide to [custom short links for your brand](/blog/custom-short-links-for-your-brand) explains the setup.

### UTM tags that reach your analytics

The short link counts clicks. Google Analytics only knows which campaign brought a visitor if the destination URL carries UTM parameters. Tag the destination, then shorten it. You can tag one link or a whole list in our free [UTM builder](/utm-builder) without an account, and our [UTM naming convention](/blog/utm-naming-convention) template keeps the tags consistent across a team.

### One link per channel

If the same link goes into your newsletter, your Instagram bio and a printed flyer, you can't tell which one worked. Make one short link for each placement, each with its own `utm_source` and `utm_medium`, and compare clicks side by side. For print, put the link in a QR code; the [free QR code generator](/qr-code-generator) makes one without sign-up.

Customer story: [how AIRO cut its rack card spend](/customers/airo). AIRO's small marketing team tracks traffic from each source and partner with iShortn, and uses the numbers to hold partners to the visits they promised.

### Links you can edit later

Campaign pages move and offers end. If the link is already in an email or on a poster, you need to change where it points without changing the link. Check how many edits a plan allows, because some tools count them.

### History long enough for reporting

Thirty days of click data is enough to check last week's post. It isn't enough for quarterly or year-on-year reports.

## How to set up a campaign link

1. **Build the tagged URL.** For example `https://yourshop.com/sale?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=winter-sale`.
2. **Shorten it on your own domain** with a readable alias, such as `go.yourshop.com/winter`.
3. **Make one variant per placement.** `go.yourshop.com/winter-ig` for Instagram, `go.yourshop.com/winter-flyer` for the QR code on your flyer, each with its own `utm_source`.
4. **Group them.** On iShortn, add the links to a campaign to see their clicks together. Pro campaigns can stamp default UTM values onto every link you add, so you don't retype them.
5. **Read both reports.** Use the shortener for clicks, location and device, and Google Analytics for what visitors did on your site. If clicks are high and sessions are low, check the landing page before you blame the channel.

## Shortening affiliate links: what's allowed

Shortening an affiliate link is common and usually fine. A short link is easier to read out in a video and tidier in a caption, and you get click counts for each placement. Three rules keep it that way.

### 1. Read your program's link rules first

Programs set their own terms. The [Amazon Associates Program Policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies) say you "will not use a link shortening service, button, hyperlink or other ad placement in a manner that makes it unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site." They also prohibit cloaking or otherwise obscuring the site a click comes from. Other programs have their own versions, so check each one before you shorten.

### 2. Disclose the relationship next to the link

In the US, the FTC's guidance for affiliate marketers says to disclose the relationship "clearly and conspicuously," and that "the closer the disclosure is to your recommendation, the better." It also says a label like "affiliate link" may not be clear enough for readers, and suggests wording such as "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post." Source: [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).

### 3. Don't hide where the link goes

A branded short link on your own domain tells people who sent it. Saying where it leads ("my Amazon list: go.yourname.com/desk") keeps you within rules like Amazon's and earns more trust than a mystery link.

iShortn has a link cloaking option on Ultra that opens the destination inside a frame, so the address bar keeps showing your short URL. Use it for your own pages, not to hide an affiliate destination. Programs such as Amazon prohibit that, and some sites refuse to load inside a frame anyway.

Links to spam, phishing or malware break iShortn's [terms](/terms), and we block them. Every destination is checked against threat lists when you create or edit a link.

### Can I use a URL shortener for affiliate links?

Usually, yes, as long as you follow the affiliate program's terms and disclose the relationship. Some programs restrict how links can be shortened. Amazon Associates doesn't allow a shortener to be used in a way that makes it unclear the link goes to Amazon.

### Should I cloak affiliate links?

Not if your program forbids it, and many do. Amazon's policy prohibits cloaking or obscuring the source of a click. A short link on your own domain with a clear label is the safer choice.
