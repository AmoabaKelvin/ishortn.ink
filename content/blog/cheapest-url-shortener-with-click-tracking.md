## What to check before you pick the cheapest one

### Total links or links per month

This is the difference people miss most often. Short.io counts links over the life of the account: 1,000 on Free and 2,500 on Hobby. iShortn, TinyURL, Bitly, Rebrandly, Cutt.ly and Dub reset your allowance every month.

If you create 10 links a week, Short.io's free plan lasts about two years before you need to delete links or upgrade. If you create 50 a week, Hobby's 2,500 runs out in about a year. A monthly allowance never runs out that way.

### How long you can see your clicks

Cheap plans often keep clicks for a short window. Bitly Core and Cutt.ly Starter show 30 days. That's fine for checking whether a post worked last week. It isn't enough to compare this December's campaign with last December's. TinyURL Pro keeps 2 years, Dub Pro keeps 1 year, and iShortn Pro keeps detailed click data for a year and daily totals for as long as you have the account.

### What happens when you go over

Going over a tracking limit doesn't break your links on these three plans. What changes is the data you get:

- **iShortn** stops recording new clicks until your allowance resets on the 1st of the month, and emails you at 80%, 90% and 100%.
- **Short.io** says its free plan keeps collecting data after 50,000 clicks but limits what you can see until you upgrade.
- **Rebrandly** gives detailed data on a set number of clicks a month (100 on Free) and counts the rest without the detail.

### The price of your own domain

If you want links on `go.yourbrand.com`, the cheapest plan with tracking may not be the cheapest plan with a domain. Bitly Core has no custom domain, and Bitly's pricing page lists it from Growth at $29 a month billed yearly ($35 monthly). Short.io, Cutt.ly, Dub and Rebrandly include a custom domain on Free, though Rebrandly Free allows only 10 new links a month. iShortn includes 3 from Pro at $8. Our guide to [custom short links for your brand](/blog/custom-short-links-for-your-brand) explains the setup.

### Whether you can change a link after sharing it

A link you can't edit costs you money later if the page moves. Short.io lists destination editing on every plan. iShortn lets you edit destinations on every plan too, Free included. Rebrandly Essentials allows 10 edits a month. Bitly adds link redirects from Core. Cutt.ly's free plan doesn't list editable redirects, and Starter allows them on the same domain.
