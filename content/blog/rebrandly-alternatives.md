Rebrandly did a lot to make branded short links normal. If you wanted `go.yourbrand.com/offer` instead of a random string on someone else's domain, it was the tool people recommended. Its free plan is small now: 10 new links a month, detailed stats on 100 clicks a month, and no way to change where a link points after you create it. The first paid plan that lets you edit links caps you at 10 edits a month.

If you share links every week, or print QR codes you might need to update, you'll hit those limits quickly. This guide covers what Rebrandly's plans include today, four alternatives with what each gives you for free and what it costs when you need more, and how to move your links.

We make iShortn, one of the alternatives. Every competitor fact here comes from that company's own pricing or help pages, checked on 21 November 2026. Prices change, so check the live pages before you decide.

## What Rebrandly's plans include

From [Rebrandly's pricing page](https://www.rebrandly.com/pricing):

| | Free | Essentials | Professional | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price (billed annually) | $0 | $11/mo | $32/mo | $149/mo |
| Price (billed monthly) | $0 | $16/mo | $39/mo | $179/mo |
| New links a month | 10 | 250 | 1,500 | 3,500 |
| New QR codes a month | 10 (watermarked) | 250 | 1,500 | 3,500 |
| Custom domains | 1 | 2 | 3 | 10 |
| Destination edits a month | None | 10 | 50 | 150 |
| Clicks with detailed stats a month | 100 | 10,000 | 25,000 | 150,000 |
| Teammates | None | None | 2+ | 5+ |

A few things are worth knowing:

- **Free includes one custom domain.** The plan comparison lists 1 domain on Free and ticks "Connect domains you already own". That slot can hold a domain you own or a free `.bio` domain from Rebrandly (the plan card's "Yourbrand.bio"), not both. Links on the `rebrand.ly` domain are included as well. A second custom domain starts on Essentials.
- **Clicks still redirect past 100.** The free plan lists unlimited clicks and scans. The 100 limit is on clicks you get detailed stats for.
- **Edits are limited.** Free has none, and each paid plan has a monthly allowance. On Essentials that's 10 edits a month across all your links and QR codes. A code you can't edit is a problem when a page moves or a promotion ends.
- **The prices may differ for you.** The pricing page's code also contains a second set of prices (for example Essentials at $8 a month annually), which suggests Rebrandly is testing prices. We've listed what the page showed us.
- **The API is available on Free**, at up to 100 calls a minute.

A banner on the same page says Rebrandly and IntelligenceBank have merged to form Radiate. Its higher tiers are built for larger marketing teams, with conversion tracking, workspaces and enterprise controls.

**Watch for:** if you left Rebrandly because 100 tracked clicks wasn't enough, TinyURL Free tracks none. Our [TinyURL alternatives](/blog/tinyurl-alternatives) guide covers it in more detail.

## How to move your links off Rebrandly

Moving is mostly about one question: **whose domain are your links on?**

### If your links use your own custom domain

You're in the best position. Your bios, emails and printed QR codes point at your domain, not at Rebrandly, so once the new service has the same short paths, every existing link keeps working.

1. **Export your links.** Rebrandly's help center says [links export is available only via API](https://support.rebrandly.com/en/articles/469598-can-i-move-my-links-to-a-new-domain) and points to a script in its developer documentation. If you have a handful of links, copying them into a spreadsheet by hand is quicker.
2. **Prepare the import file.** iShortn's CSV import reads `url` (the destination), `alias` (the short path, such as `offer` for `go.yourbrand.com/offer`), and optionally `domain` and `note`.
3. **Add your domain to the new service first** (on iShortn, under custom domains on Pro), then import with that domain in the `domain` column so the links are created on it.
4. **Switch DNS** to the new provider's records. Do it at a quiet time and test a few links afterwards.
5. **Keep your old analytics.** Click history doesn't move between providers. Rebrandly lets paid plans [export analytics as CSV](https://support.rebrandly.com/en/articles/469642-can-i-export-analytics-data-as-a-csv-file).

Imported links count toward your monthly link allowance, so a big import may need a paid plan for the month you move.

### If your links use `rebrand.ly` or a domain Rebrandly gave you

Only Rebrandly controls where those links redirect, so they can't be moved. Create new links elsewhere, update the places you control (bio, website, email signature, pinned posts), and leave the old links live. Use the move to set up a domain you own, so you never have to do this again. Our guide to [custom short links for your brand](/blog/custom-short-links-for-your-brand) walks through it.

## When to stay with Rebrandly

Rebrandly makes sense for bigger teams. If you need conversion tracking, several workspaces, many teammates or enterprise controls, its Professional and Growth plans cover that and iShortn doesn't fully replace them yet. If you only need a few links a month and Rebrandly's free API, it may still be enough.

If your use is simpler (branded links, QR codes you can update, honest click stats), you don't need to pay per edit.
