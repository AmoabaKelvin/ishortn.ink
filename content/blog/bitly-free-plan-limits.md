If you've just hit a "monthly limit reached" message, or you're wondering why your Bitly QR code has no scan numbers, this guide lists every limit on Bitly Free today, what happens when you reach one, and what to use if the free plan no longer works for you.

## Bitly free plan limits at a glance

| What | Bitly Free |
|---|---|
| Short links | 5 a month |
| QR codes | 2 a month |
| Custom back-halves | 3 a month |
| Landing pages | 2 |
| Clicks and QR scans | Unlimited |
| Click and scan analytics | None |
| Change a link's destination | No |
| Custom domain | No, starts on Growth at $29/month |
| Ads | An ad page before the link opens |
| API | 1,000 requests a month |

Two details in Bitly's help article ["What are my monthly limits?"](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/11243274022413-What-are-my-monthly-limits) matter more than the headline numbers.

First, QR codes and landing page links count toward your short-link total. Make 2 QR codes and you have 3 short links left that month, not 5.

Second, the limits reset at midnight UTC at the end of each calendar month, according to ["When do my monthly limits reset?"](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/10796542543373-When-do-my-monthly-limits-reset). Unused links don't roll over. If you hit the cap on the 3rd, you wait until the 1st or you upgrade.

## Is there a Bitly QR code scan limit?

No. Bitly lists "unlimited clicks and QR Code scans" on every plan, including Free. A Bitly QR code won't stop scanning because too many people used it, and Bitly's blog says free-plan links don't expire.

The limits people run into with Bitly QR codes on Free are different ones:

- **You can only make 2 a month,** and each one uses up one of your 5 links.
- **You can't see scans.** Bitly's blog says the free plan doesn't include click or scan analytics for short links or QR codes. You'll know the code works, but not whether 3 people scanned it or 3,000.
- **You can't change where it points.** Bitly calls editing a destination a "redirect", and Free includes none. If the page behind a printed code moves, the code keeps sending people to the old address, and the fix is a new code and a reprint.

That last point catches small businesses the most. A menu, a flyer or a shop window sticker usually outlives the URL it was printed with. We wrote more about this in [why QR codes stop working](/blog/qr-code-stopped-working).

## The ad page on free links

People who open a link made on a free Bitly account see a Bitly page first. It previews the destination and shows an ad, and the visitor has to continue from there. Bitly's help article ["Why are there ads on my links?"](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/32874287800333-Why-are-there-ads-on-my-links), updated 31 August 2026, says ads fund the free plan and that paid plans remove them.

For a personal link that's a small annoyance. For a business, it means the first thing a customer sees after tapping your link is someone else's ad.

## What happens when you hit a limit

From Bitly's help center:

- When you reach your link limit, you can't create new links until the next calendar month starts.
- Links you've already made keep working. Clicks and scans aren't capped.
- If you were on a paid plan and downgrade to Free, your bit.ly links keep working. Links on a custom domain keep redirecting as long as the domain's DNS still points at Bitly, but Bitly stops renewing the SSL certificate, so visitors may see a "not secure" warning later. If you used Bitly's complimentary domain, those links stop working when its registration runs out ([downgrade article](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029870551-What-happens-when-I-downgrade-to-a-lower-subscription-plan)). Planning to leave? [How to cancel Bitly without breaking your links](/blog/cancel-bitly-subscription) covers what to export and move first.

## Where Bitly is still the better choice

Bitly is a good product, and for some people it's the right one:

- **You use the API on a free account.** Bitly Free includes 1,000 API requests a month. iShortn's API is on Pro and Ultra only.
- **You need a big company's extras.** Bitly's higher tiers add mobile deep linking, SSO and an uptime SLA, and iShortn doesn't offer those.
- **Your organisation already standardises on Bitly.** Switching costs time, and links on bit.ly can't move with you.

That last point is worth thinking about before you switch. Links on `bit.ly` belong to Bitly's domain, so you can't move them anywhere. They'll keep working, but new links will live elsewhere. ## FAQ

### How many links can I make on Bitly for free?

5 short links a month, as of November 2026. QR codes and landing page links count toward the 5, and the limit resets at the end of each calendar month, midnight UTC.

### Does Bitly limit QR code scans?

No. Scans are unlimited on every plan. The free plan limits how many QR codes you can create, 2 a month, and it doesn't show scan counts or let you change the destination.

### Do Bitly free links expire?

Bitly says links made on the free plan don't expire. They do show an ad page before redirecting.

### Can I edit a Bitly link on the free plan?

You can't change where a link points on Bitly Free. Changing destinations, which Bitly calls redirects, starts on Core, with 5 a month.

### What's the cheapest Bitly plan with analytics?

Core, at $10 a month billed annually. It keeps 30 days of click data.
