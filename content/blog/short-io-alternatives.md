Short.io costs nothing to start: its free plan includes 5 custom domains, 1,000 links in total and 50,000 tracked clicks a month. Paid plans are Hobby at $5 a month, Pro at $18, Team at $48 and Enterprise at $148, with 17% off if you pay yearly. You'll want an alternative if you hit the 1,000-link lifetime cap, need a Pro feature such as password protection, or need a second person on the account, which Short.io only allows from Team.

For a lot of people the free plan is enough, and they should stay. This guide covers what each Short.io plan includes, when to stay, and which alternatives cost less for the things Short.io charges more for.

We make iShortn, one of the alternatives below. Every competitor fact comes from that company's own pricing page, checked on 21 November 2026. Prices change, so check the live pages before you decide.

## What Short.io costs

From [Short.io's pricing page](https://short.io/pricing), monthly prices:

| | Free | Hobby | Pro | Team | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $5/mo | $18/mo | $48/mo | $148/mo |
| Users | 1 | 1 | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Custom domains | 5 | 7 | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Branded links | 1,000 in total | 2,500 in total | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Tracked clicks | 50,000/mo | 100,000/mo | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Added on this plan | | Country targeting, referrer hiding | Password protection, link expiration, link cloaking | City and region targeting, mobile deep links, 99.9% uptime SLA | SSO, multiple teams, raw data export to S3 |

Every plan includes destination editing, the API, QR codes, a UTM builder, link import and export, A/B testing and mobile targeting. Paid plans come with a 7-day free trial, and Short.io allows commercial use on Free.

Two details matter more than the prices:

- **The link limits on Free and Hobby are lifetime totals.** You can delete old links to make room. Short.io suggests setting a 404 redirect first so old links still go somewhere.
- **Free and Hobby show a set number of clicks in detail.** Short.io's FAQ says the free plan keeps collecting clicks after 50,000 but limits what you can see until you upgrade. Your links keep working either way.

## When to stay with Short.io

Stay if you need several custom domains and don't want to pay, if you make fewer than about 1,000 links over the life of the account, or if you rely on the API or A/B testing on a free or cheap plan. If your team is more than a few people and needs SSO, Short.io Enterprise and Bitly's Teams plan are built for that, and iShortn isn't.

## Why people look for a Short.io alternative

- **The lifetime link cap.** If you post three links a day, 1,000 links in total runs out in under a year. Hobby only raises it to 2,500.
- **Password protection and link expiry cost $18.** Both are Pro features on Short.io.
- **One user until Team at $48.** A freelancer and a client, or two people at a small business, can't share Free, Hobby or Pro.

## How to move your links off Short.io

What you can move depends on whose domain your links use.

**If they're on your own domain**, you can move without breaking anything. Your printed QR codes and old posts point at your domain, so once the new service has the same short paths, they keep working.

1. Export your links. Short.io's pricing page lists link export on every plan, and its FAQ says support can prepare an export for you.
2. On iShortn, add your domain under custom domains (Pro and up).
3. Import a CSV with `url` (the destination), `alias` (the short path, such as `offer` for `go.yourbrand.com/offer`) and `domain`. CSV import is on Pro and Ultra, and imported links count toward the monthly link allowance.
4. Point your domain's DNS at iShortn at a quiet time, then test a few links.

Click history doesn't move between services, so download any reports you want to keep before you switch.

**If they're on a domain Short.io gave you**, only Short.io controls those redirects. Leave them live, make new links on your own domain, and update your bio, website and pinned posts.
