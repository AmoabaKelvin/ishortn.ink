You can add up to 5 links to your Instagram bio without paying anything. Go to your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Links**, then **Add link**, and repeat for each link. If you need more than 5, you have two options: Meta's paid Meta One plans raise the limit to 20 links, or you can put a single link in your bio that opens a link-in-bio page holding as many links as you need.

This guide covers each option, what it costs and who it suits. Instagram's limits come from [Meta's help center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/29142271265407659/), checked on 22 November 2026.

## How to add up to 5 links to your Instagram bio

These are the steps from Meta's help center, in the Instagram app:

1. Tap **Profile** or your profile picture in the bottom right.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Links**.
4. Tap **Add link**.
5. Enter the URL and a title for the link, then tap **Done**.

Repeat steps 4 and 5 for each link, up to 5. Meta's page doesn't limit the 5 links to business or creator accounts.

Some habits that help:

- **Add the most important link first.** Meta's help page says links appear in the order you add them, so put the one you care about most at the top.
- **Write titles people understand.** Meta's own advice is that "a short description works better than the web address". "Book a table" tells people more than "mybistro.com/reservations".
- **Delete links you no longer need.** A link to last month's sale is a wasted tap.

## When 5 links aren't enough

The built-in links are fine for many accounts. You'll want something else if one of these applies:

- **You need more than 5 links.** Shops with several collections, musicians with a release on every streaming service, and creators with a shop, a newsletter and several partner codes run out fast.
- **You want to know which links get tapped.** If you're choosing between promoting the shop or the newsletter, click counts per link settle it.
- **You share the same links on TikTok, YouTube and in your email signature.** One page to update beats editing five profiles every time something changes.
- **You change links often.** Weekly offers, launches and event sign-ups mean constant edits.

## Option 1: Meta One, for up to 20 links inside Instagram

Meta's help center says a Meta One subscription lets you "add up to 20 links, add an image and a title to each one, and share your link page anywhere using its own URL". The link page opens without people leaving Instagram. It's part of the Meta One Essential, Advanced, Expert and Max plans, and Meta's announcement lists Essential as "starting at $14.99/mo". Teens can't use it.

It makes sense if you want Meta One's other features anyway, such as the verified badge that comes with Essential. If all you want is more links, $14.99 a month is a lot to pay for a link page when free link-in-bio pages exist. Links in organic posts and reels need Meta One Advanced, which Meta lists from $49.99 a month.

## Option 2: A link-in-bio page

A link-in-bio page is a small mobile page with a button for each link. You put its address in your Instagram bio, usually as the first of your 5 links, and add or change buttons on the page without touching your profile. Most tools count how many people view the page and tap each button.

Linktree is the best-known one, and its free plan allows unlimited links. Beacons, Carrd and others cover the same ground in different ways. We compare prices, free plans and where each one is better in our guide to [Linktree alternatives](/blog/linktree-alternatives).

A bio page suits you if you have more than 5 links, change them often or want the same page in every bio. It's overkill for one or two links. The page adds a tap, so if you only ever send people to your shop, link straight to the shop.

## Option 3: Keep Instagram's 5 links, and track each one

If 5 links are enough and you just want to know which ones work, you don't need a bio page. Turn each of your 5 links into a tracked short link and paste those into Instagram instead.

With a short link, you can:

- **See clicks per link**, with the country and device behind each click.
- **Change where a link goes without editing your profile.** Point "Weekend menu" at a new page on Friday, and the link in your bio stays the same.
- **Use the same link elsewhere**, so you can compare Instagram with your other channels.

## Quick answers

**How many links can you put in an Instagram bio?** Up to 5 for free. Meta One subscribers can add up to 20 on a link page.

**Do I need a business account to add links?** Meta's help center doesn't say so. It gives the 5-link limit without naming an account type.

**Can I add links to Instagram posts?** Not on a regular account. Meta lists links in organic posts and reels as a Meta One Advanced feature, from $49.99 a month.

**Is a link-in-bio page better than Instagram's 5 links?** Only if you need more than 5 links, use the same links on other platforms, or want click counts per link without converting each link yourself.

**Can I see how many people tap my Instagram links?** Yes, if the links are tracked short links or buttons on a link-in-bio page. Each tap counts as a click.
