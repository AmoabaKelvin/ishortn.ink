Monthly Pro and Ultra subscribers now see a small card offering yearly billing, with the saving spelled out.

## Pay yearly, get two months free

- **Ultra:** $150 a year instead of $180 paid monthly, a saving of $30.
- **Pro:** $80 a year instead of $96 paid monthly, a saving of $16.

Select **Switch to annual**, check the summary in the confirmation dialog, and confirm. The change goes through the same checkout as **Settings > Billing**, where you can also switch at any time.

## Where you'll see it

The card appears on the dashboard home and above the Billing card in Settings, for personal workspaces on a monthly plan that is at least 30 days old. It stays hidden while another notice (such as a promotion) is showing, and you can dismiss it; it won't come back for 60 days. Subscribers on the older $5 Pro plan don't see it, because yearly Pro would cost them more.
