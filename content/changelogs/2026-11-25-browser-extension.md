iShortn now has a browser extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge. It's free, and you can [get it here](/extension).

## Shorten the page you're on

Click the iShortn icon in your toolbar, or press **Alt+Shift+S**, and select **Shorten & copy**. The page you're on becomes a short link and lands on your clipboard. On Pro and Ultra the extension shortens the page as soon as the popup opens. You can switch this on or off on any plan with **Shorten automatically when I open the popup**.

Open **Customize** to set your own alias or pick a domain, including your custom domains on Pro and Ultra.

## A QR code for every link

Each link comes with a QR code you can download as a PNG. Before you sign in, the popup still shows a QR code for the page you're on, made on your device.

## Your recent links

The popup lists your five latest links with a copy button, and shows how many links you have left this month. On browser pages that can't be shortened, such as settings pages, you can paste a link instead.

## Sign in once, no API key

Select **Connect iShortn** in the popup and approve the connection on ishortn.ink. Links go into your personal workspace with the same click analytics as links made in the dashboard. To disconnect a browser, go to **Settings** and use **Connected browsers**, or select **Sign out** in the extension.

The extension reads the address of the current tab only when you click its icon or press the shortcut. It can't see page content, your other tabs or your browsing history.

## Installing on Edge

Edge installs the extension from the Chrome Web Store. Open [the extension page](/extension), and when Edge asks, select **Allow extensions from other stores**, then **Add to Chrome**.
