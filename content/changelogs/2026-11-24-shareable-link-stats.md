A public stats page for any link, on every plan, and a sign-up page that remembers what you came to shorten.

## Share a link's stats with a public page

You can now give anyone a read-only page with a link's analytics. It's useful for sending results to a client or a manager without giving them an account.

There are four ways to open the sharing settings:

- **Share**, next to **Export CSV** on a link's analytics page.
- **Share stats** in the actions menu on a link card.
- **Share**, next to the date range selector on a QR code's detail page.
- **Share stats** in the actions menu on a QR code card, for QR codes with a tracking link.

A QR code shares the same public page as its tracking link, so turning sharing on or off in either place changes both.

Turn on **Anyone with the link can view these stats** and the page's URL appears with a **Copy** button. **Reset link** gives you a new URL, and the old one stops working. Turning the switch off revokes the page straight away.

People with the URL see:

- the short link and the link's name
- total and unique visits
- the clicks chart
- countries
- devices, browsers and operating systems
- referrer sources

They never see the destination URL, who owns the link, notes, tags, cities or individual visits. If the link is disabled, the page stops working too.

Shared pages for Free accounts show the last 7 days. On Pro and Ultra, viewers can switch between the date range presets, from the last 24 hours to all time.

In a team workspace, team admins and the person who created the link can change sharing. Other members can see whether it's on.

## Sign-up keeps the link you were creating

If you paste a URL into the shortener on the homepage and then sign up, the sign-up page now opens with a **Get your short link** heading and a card showing the link you entered. The form also appears sooner on slow connections.

## Free analytics count the clicks outside the 7-day window

On the Free plan, a link's analytics page, the analytics overview and a QR code's page now say how many older clicks fall outside the last 7 days, for example "1,284 older clicks are hidden on Free (last 7 days shown)." On the overview, the count covers the links in the current view, so a folder or tag filter applies. Pro and Ultra show the full history.
