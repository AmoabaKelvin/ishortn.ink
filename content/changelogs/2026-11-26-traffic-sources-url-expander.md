## Fewer clicks stuck in "Direct"

Many apps open links in their own built-in browser and send no referrer, so those clicks used to show up as **Direct**. iShortn now recognises the in-app browsers of Instagram, Facebook and Messenger, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, Telegram, WeChat and LINE, and credits the click to that platform.

Clicks on a QR code link that arrive without a referrer are now counted as **QR code scan**.

The **Traffic sources** card, on each link's analytics and on the analytics overview, has new tabs: **All**, **Channels**, **Social**, **Email** and **Search**. **Channels** groups your clicks into social, email, search, messaging, QR, websites and direct, so you can see at a glance where people find your links. Hover the info icon next to **Direct** to see what still ends up there.

This applies to new clicks from today. Older clicks keep their original source.

## Free URL expander

Not sure where a short link goes? Paste it into the new [URL expander](/url-expander) to see the final destination and every redirect on the way, before you click. It's free and needs no account.

## Clearer text in dark mode

We raised the contrast of muted text, labels and placeholders across the dashboard and public pages so they're easier to read in dark mode. Thanks to the community member who reported it.
