Four changes to pricing, billing and link safety.

## Annual plans shown first

The pricing page, the in-app pricing page and Settings → Billing now open on annual billing and show the per-month price: Pro is $6.67/mo billed $80 yearly, Ultra is $12.50/mo billed $150 yearly. You can still switch to monthly with one click. If you already pay monthly, these pages open on monthly so your current plan still shows as current. The pricing FAQ now explains what happens when you switch between monthly and annual.

## Paused tracking is flagged across the dashboard

When a Free or Pro account goes over its monthly events and tracking pauses, a banner now appears across the dashboard, not just on link and bio-page analytics. It shows when tracking paused, how many visits went through without being recorded, and when it resets. You can dismiss it for 24 hours. The sidebar meter also warns you once you've used 80% of your events.

## Failed payments go straight to your card details

If a renewal payment fails, you keep your plan for 7 days. A dashboard banner and reminder emails tell you when your access ends and link to Lemon Squeezy's update-payment page for your existing subscription, so you can fix the card without starting a new plan. If the failed payment is for Ultra and you own a team, the team workspace becomes read-only after those 7 days unless the payment method is updated. Existing links keep working.

## Deleted links can't be taken over

On our shared domains, an alias you delete or rename is now retired for good. Nobody can create a new link with it later and pick up the traffic from your old QR codes, posts or printed material. Custom domains are unaffected.
