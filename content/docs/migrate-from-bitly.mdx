---
title: Move your links from Bitly
description: Bring your Bitly links, branded domain and printed QR codes to iShortn. Export from Bitly, connect your domain, import the file and check that old links still work.
---

You can move your Bitly links to iShortn by importing Bitly's link export as it is, without editing the file. Links on your own domain, such as `go.yourbrand.com/menu`, keep their exact addresses: printed flyers, menus and QR codes keep working once your domain points to iShortn.

## What moves and what doesn't

| | Moves | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Links on your own domain | Yes, same address | Custom back-halves become aliases on your domain. |
| Bitly's random codes on your domain, such as `go.yourbrand.com/3XCetqn` | Yes, same address | Optional, on by default. Each one counts as a link. |
| Titles | Yes | Become the link name. |
| Links on `bit.ly` | Destination only | `bit.ly` belongs to Bitly, so those addresses can't move. The import creates a new short link for each one and lists the new address. |
| Links on Bitly's complimentary domain | No | Bitly registered that domain for you and says it can't be transferred. Links on it keep working until the registration expires. |
| Tags, click history, created dates | No | Bitly's export has no tags. Analytics start fresh from the first click on iShortn. |
| Links deleted in Bitly | No | Rows marked `Deleted` are skipped. |

A QR code keeps working after the move only if it opens an address on your own domain. Scan one of your printed codes and look at the address before you start.

## Before you start

- **An iShortn Pro or Ultra plan.** CSV import and custom domains are on paid plans. Pro includes 3 custom domains, 1,000 new links a month and a one-time allowance of 5,000 imported links on your own domains; Ultra has no link limit. See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans).
- **A Bitly plan with data export.** Bitly lists data export on Growth and higher. Bitly Free and Core don't have custom domains, so their links all sit on `bit.ly` and can't keep their addresses anyway.
- **Access to your domain's DNS**, at the company where you manage the domain.
- **About 15 minutes**, at a quiet time for your links. Links on your domain stop redirecting from the moment you change the DNS until the import finishes. That's usually a few minutes.

## 1. Export your links from Bitly

1. In Bitly, open **Links** in the left sidebar and click **Export**.
2. Choose an email address and click **Export links**.
3. Open the email from Bitly (from team@accounts.bitly.com; large exports can take up to 30 minutes) and click **View Report** to download the CSV.

Selecting links and exporting them gives at most 100 links, so use the full export above. Keep your Bitly account until you've checked the move: it's your way back if something goes wrong.

If the file has more than 1,000 links, split it into files of up to 1,000 rows. Each file needs the header row.

## 2. Add your domain to iShortn

1. Open [Domains](/dashboard/domains) and click **Add Domain**.
2. Enter the same domain you use on Bitly, for example `go.yourbrand.com`.
3. Leave the page open. The domain card shows the DNS records to create.

Full details are in [Custom domains](/docs/custom-domains).

## 3. Point the domain to iShortn

In your DNS provider, replace Bitly's records with the ones on the iShortn domain card.

| Your Bitly setup | Remove | Add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subdomain, such as `go.yourbrand.com` | `CNAME` to `cname.bitly.com` | `CNAME` to `cname.ishortn.ink`, plus the `TXT` record from the domain card |
| Whole domain, such as `yourbrand.co` | The two `A` records to `67.199.248.12` and `67.199.248.13` | An `ALIAS` or `ANAME` record (or a flattened `CNAME`) to `cname.ishortn.ink`, plus the `TXT` record from the domain card |

If your DNS is on Cloudflare, set the record to **DNS only** (grey cloud). If your DNS provider has no ALIAS, ANAME or flattened CNAME records (GoDaddy is the common one), move the domain's DNS to Cloudflare's free plan first. See [Apex domain on GoDaddy or a similar provider](/docs/custom-domains#apex-domain-on-a-provider-without-alias-records). Do it before you change anything else: Cloudflare copies Bitly's records across, so your links keep working while the nameservers change.

Back in iShortn, click **Check Now** until the domain shows **Active**. DNS changes usually show up within minutes.

## 4. Import the file

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard), open the **…** menu next to **New Link** and choose **Upload CSV**.
2. Drop in the file from Bitly, unchanged.
3. Leave **Also keep Bitly's original codes working** on if any of those codes were shared or printed.
4. Click **Import links**.

The summary lists anything that needs attention:

- **"… isn't connected to this workspace"**: the domain isn't Active yet, or it was added in another workspace. Finish step 3, then import the same file again.
- **"bit.ly links stay on Bitly. Created …"**: a new short link was made for a `bit.ly` link. Use the new address from now on.
- **"Alias already exists"**: another link on that domain uses the alias. Aliases ignore letter case, so `Menu` and `menu` are the same alias.

You can import the same file again after fixing a problem. Links that already came across are counted as already imported, and only real errors are listed.

## 5. Check that it worked

1. Open three or four of your old links in a private browser window, including one with a custom back-half and one random code.
2. Scan a printed QR code with your phone.
3. Open [Analytics](/dashboard) and confirm those visits appear.

When everything redirects, you can cancel Bitly. Until then, putting Bitly's DNS records back undoes the move.

## Questions

### Do my printed QR codes need reprinting?

Not if they open an address on your own domain. They point to the same address, and that address now redirects through iShortn. Codes that open a `bit.ly` address stay on Bitly.

### Can I bring my Bitly tags?

Not from the CSV, because Bitly's export doesn't include them. Add tags to the links from the dashboard after the import.

### I have more links than my monthly limit

On Pro, links imported onto your own domain first use a one-time allowance of 5,000 links, so they don't count toward your [monthly link limit](/docs/short-links#monthly-link-limit). Links created for `bit.ly` rows, and any rows after the allowance is used up, count toward the monthly limit of 1,000. Ultra has no limit. Rows past the limit are listed in the summary; import the same file again next month, or upgrade to Ultra. See [CSV bulk import](/docs/bulk-import#limits) for details.
