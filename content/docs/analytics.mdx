Every short link, QR code and bio page comes with its own analytics. There is nothing to install: each visit is recorded as the visitor is redirected.

## What's recorded

For each click, iShortn records:

- **When**: the date and time of the click.
- **Where**: country, city and continent, derived from the visitor's IP address.
- **Device**: device type (mobile, tablet, desktop), device model, operating system and browser.
- **Referrer**: where the click came from, such as `twitter`, `linkedin` or `direct` when there's no referrer.
- **Targeting**: which [targeting rule](/docs/short-links#geo-and-device-targeting), if any, matched the visit.
- **Verified**: whether the click was [verified](/docs/short-links#verified-clicks) as coming from a real browser.

**Unique visitors** are counted with a one-way hash of the visitor's IP address; the IP address itself isn't stored. Clicks from known bots and crawlers aren't recorded.

## Dashboard views

Click a link in the dashboard to open its analytics:

- Total, unique and verified clicks, each compared with the previous period.
- Clicks over time.
- Countries and cities. Pro and Ultra also see continents and a world map.
- Browsers, devices, models and operating systems.
- Referrers.
- Clicks per targeting rule.

The [analytics overview](/dashboard/analytics/overview) combines every link in the workspace.

## How long data is kept

| | Free | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detailed click data | 30 days | 365 days | Kept |
| Daily click totals | Kept | Kept | Kept |

When detailed click data passes its retention window, it's rolled up into a daily total of clicks and unique clicks per link, then removed. Your long-term trend (clicks over time) survives; the breakdowns by country, device and so on cover only the detailed data that's still kept. Links in a [team workspace](/docs/teams) follow the team owner's plan. Bio page views follow the same windows.

## Tracked clicks

Clicks, QR scans and bio page views count as **tracked clicks**. Each plan includes a monthly allowance:

| | Free | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tracked clicks per month | <PlanValue plan="free" cap="eventsLimit" /> | <PlanValue plan="pro" cap="eventsLimit" /> | <PlanValue plan="ultra" cap="eventsLimit" /> |

You get an email at 80%, 90% and 100% of the allowance. Past 100%, **links keep redirecting**, but new visits aren't recorded until the allowance resets on the first day of the next month. The analytics pages show a banner while tracking is paused.

## Export

- **Links**: export your link list as CSV or JSON from the dashboard's bulk actions menu. The file has each link's creation date, destination, alias, domain and note.
- **Clicks** (Pro and Ultra): **Export CSV** on a link's analytics page or the analytics overview downloads every click in the selected date range (and overview filter), newest first: timestamp (UTC), short link, destination, country, city, device, browser, OS and referrer. One export holds up to 100,000 clicks; pick a shorter range for older ones. Clicks past the retention window exist only as daily totals, so they aren't in the file.
- **Campaigns**: export a campaign's analytics as CSV from the campaign page.
- **API**: fetch a link's full analytics as JSON with [Link analytics](/docs/api/link-analytics).

## Scheduled email reports

Send a link's, a folder's or a campaign's numbers to people by email on a schedule. Click **Email report** on the link's analytics page, the folder page or the campaign page, add up to 5 email addresses and pick a frequency:

- **Weekly**: every Monday, covering the previous Monday to Sunday (UTC).
- **Monthly**: on the 1st, covering the previous calendar month.

Each report has total clicks and the change from the period before, top countries, devices and traffic sources, and for folders and campaigns the top links. If the link's stats are shared, the email links to the live stats page. Recipients don't need an iShortn account, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

| | Free | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled reports per workspace | None | 3 | Unlimited |

Team workspaces have no limit. If a workspace has more schedules than its plan allows (for example after a downgrade), the oldest ones keep sending and the rest pause until you upgrade.
