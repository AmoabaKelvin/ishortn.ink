iShortn has three plans. Free needs no credit card; Pro and Ultra are billed monthly or yearly. See [Pricing](/pricing) for current prices, and upgrade from [Settings → Billing](/dashboard/settings#billing).

## Limits

These numbers are rendered from the same configuration the app enforces, so they're always current.

<PlanLimits />

[Team workspaces](/docs/teams) always get Ultra limits. Monthly limits (links and tracked clicks) reset on the first day of each calendar month.

## Data retention

| | Free | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detailed click data | 30 days | 365 days | Kept |
| Daily click totals | Kept | Kept | Kept |

See [Analytics](/docs/analytics#how-long-data-is-kept) for details.

## What happens at a limit

- **Monthly links**: new links are refused with a message such as `You've reached your monthly limit of 30 links. Upgrade to Pro for more.` Existing links keep working.
- **Monthly tracked clicks**: links keep redirecting, but new clicks aren't recorded until the next month. You're emailed at 80%, 90% and 100%.
- **Everything else** (domains, folders, bio pages, rules): the dashboard asks you to upgrade when you try to add one more.
