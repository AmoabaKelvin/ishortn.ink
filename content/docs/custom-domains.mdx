With a custom domain, your short links read `go.yourbrand.com/spring-sale` instead of `isht.app/spring-sale`. iShortn issues and renews the SSL certificate for you.

In a [team workspace](/docs/teams), only the team owner can add or remove domains.

## Pick a domain

Use a **subdomain** of a domain you own, such as `go.yourbrand.com` or `links.yourbrand.com`. Subdomains work with every DNS provider and leave your main website untouched.

You can also use a bare (apex) domain such as `yourbrand.link`, but only if nothing else is served from it and your DNS provider supports ALIAS, ANAME or flattened CNAME records. Cloudflare, Namecheap, Porkbun and Route 53 all do. If yours doesn't (GoDaddy, for example), you can move the domain's DNS to Cloudflare's free plan; see [below](#apex-domain-on-a-provider-without-alias-records).

## Add the domain

1. Open [Domains](/dashboard/domains) in the dashboard and click **Add Domain**.
2. Enter the domain, for example `links.example.com`. Leave out `https://` and `www.`; iShortn strips them anyway.
3. Confirm. The domain appears in the list as **Pending**, with the DNS records to create.

## Create the DNS records

Sign in to the company that runs DNS for your domain (often where you bought it) and add the records shown on the domain card. They follow this pattern:

| Type | Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | The subdomain part, such as `links` for `links.example.com`. `@` for an apex domain. | `cname.ishortn.ink` |
| `TXT` | `_cf-custom-hostname`, or `_cf-custom-hostname.links` for `links.example.com` | The value shown on the domain card |

The `TXT` record proves you own the domain and only appears when it's needed. Always copy the exact names and values from the domain card: they're generated for your domain.

For an apex domain, create the `CNAME` as an **ALIAS** or **ANAME** record (or a CNAME at the root, if your provider flattens it) pointing to the same value.

If your domain uses Cloudflare DNS, set the record's proxy status to **DNS only** (grey cloud) and set **SSL/TLS** to **Full**.

## Verification

iShortn checks the records automatically every 30 seconds while the Domains page is open, and you can click **Check Now** at any time. The domain becomes **Active** once both the DNS record and the SSL certificate are in place. DNS changes usually show up within minutes but can take up to 48 hours.

Once a domain is active, it appears in the domain menu when you create or edit links in that workspace. You can also make it your [default domain](/docs/platform-domains#setting-a-default).

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting for the DNS records or the certificate. |
| Active | Ready. Links on the domain redirect. |
| Invalid | Verification failed. Check the records against the domain card. |

If a domain stays misconfigured, iShortn emails a reminder at most once a week. A domain left **Invalid** for 30 days is removed.

## Troubleshooting

- **Still pending after an hour.** Compare each record with the domain card character by character. A common mistake is entering the full domain as the name (`links.example.com.example.com`); most providers add your domain automatically, so enter only `links`.
- **Cloudflare DNS.** Turn the proxy off (DNS only) on the CNAME record and set SSL/TLS to Full.
- **An apex domain won't verify.** Your DNS provider may not support CNAME-style records at the root. Use a subdomain such as `links.example.com`, or, if the domain is already printed or shared, move its DNS to Cloudflare as described below.
- **"This domain is already added to this workspace".** It's already in your list.
- **"This domain is already connected to another account".** Remove it from the other account first, or contact [support@ishortn.ink](mailto:support@ishortn.ink) if you own it.
- **Plan limit.** Pro accounts can add <PlanValue plan="pro" cap="domainLimit" /> domains. Remove one or upgrade to Ultra for more.

## Apex domain on a provider without ALIAS records

Some DNS providers, including GoDaddy, can't point a bare domain such as `yourbrand.link` at another hostname. If you need that exact domain (for example because it's already on printed QR codes), move its DNS to Cloudflare. The free plan is enough, and the domain stays registered where it is.

1. Create a free account at [cloudflare.com](https://www.cloudflare.com), click **Add a domain**, enter the domain and choose the **Free** plan.
2. Cloudflare scans your existing DNS records. Compare its list with the records at your current provider, especially `MX` and `TXT` records if the domain receives email, and add anything it missed.
3. Cloudflare shows two nameservers. At your registrar (for GoDaddy: **My Products** > the domain > **DNS** > **Nameservers** > **Change**), replace the current nameservers with Cloudflare's two.
4. Wait until Cloudflare marks the domain **Active**. This usually takes under an hour and can take up to 24 hours. Your existing records keep working in the meantime.
5. In Cloudflare DNS, replace the records that point the root elsewhere with a `CNAME` named `@` pointing to `cname.ishortn.ink`, set to **DNS only** (grey cloud). Cloudflare flattens it automatically. Add the `TXT` record from the domain card as well.
6. Back in iShortn, click **Check Now** until the domain is **Active**.

## Link-in-bio pages on your domain

On Pro and Ultra, an active custom domain can also serve a [link-in-bio page](/docs/bio-pages) at its root: `yourbrand.com` shows the page and `yourbrand.com/<alias>` keeps redirecting short links.

## Removing a domain

Deleting a domain also **deletes every link on that domain in the workspace, with its analytics**. This can't be undone. Move links you want to keep to another domain first by editing them.
