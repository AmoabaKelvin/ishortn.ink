A link-in-bio page collects your links, socials and contact details on one page you can put in an Instagram, TikTok or X bio. Every link on it is tracked. Churches and charities often put one on the bulletin code; see [QR codes for churches and nonprofits](/solutions/churches-and-nonprofits). Creators and freelancers can start with [link in bio for creators](/solutions/creators).

## Create a page

1. Open [Bio Pages](/dashboard/bio-pages) and create a new page.
2. Choose a handle. Your page lives at `ishortn.ink/p/<handle>`.
3. Add blocks, pick a theme, and publish.

Handles are 3 to 100 characters of lowercase letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, and are unique across iShortn. A few words are reserved for iShortn's own pages.

## Blocks

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Link | A button to any URL. Each link block gets its own tracked short link. |
| Heading | A section title. |
| Text | A short paragraph. |
| Social | Icons linking to your social profiles. |
| Divider | A line between sections. |
| Email | A button that opens an email to you. |
| Embed | A YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Spotify or SoundCloud player, or a Google Map. |
| Image | One uploaded image, with alt text and an optional tracked link (Pro and Ultra). |

On the Free plan, only **link** blocks and **embed** blocks are capped per page (one embed per page); headings, text, socials, dividers and email blocks are unlimited, and embeds don't count toward the link-block limit. On Ultra, any block can be scheduled to appear and disappear at set times.

Embeds load as a lightweight preview. The provider's player, and its scripts and cookies, only load when a visitor taps it, so embeds don't slow the page down. Paste the full link from the provider: short links such as `vm.tiktok.com` or `maps.app.goo.gl` can't be embedded, and neither can pages that aren't a single video, track, playlist, podcast or place.

Images on a bio page (your avatar, image blocks) must be uploaded rather than linked from another site. An image block with a link uses one link from your monthly link allowance, like any other tracked link; an image without a link doesn't.

## Themes

Every plan can pick from the curated theme presets. Pro and Ultra can customise the accent colour, background, font and button style (rounded, pill, sharp or outline), and remove the "Made with iShortn" badge.

## Use your own domain

On Pro and Ultra, a bio page can be served at the root of an active [custom domain](/docs/custom-domains): visitors to `yourbrand.com` (or `www.yourbrand.com`) see the page, while `yourbrand.com/<alias>` keeps redirecting your short links. Choose the domain in the page's settings. Platform domains such as `isht.app` can't be used for this.

## Analytics

Each bio page records views, and every link block records clicks like any other short link. Page views count toward your monthly [tracked clicks](/docs/analytics#tracked-clicks).
