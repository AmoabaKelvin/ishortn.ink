---
title: Abuse and acceptable use
description: What you can't use iShortn for, how links are checked for phishing and malware, and how to report a harmful short link.
---

iShortn links are trusted because they aren't used to hurt people. The [Terms of Service](/terms) set out what isn't allowed. In short, don't use iShortn to link to:

- Phishing pages, or pages that impersonate another person or brand.
- Malware, or downloads that harm devices.
- Spam, including links sent in unsolicited bulk messages.
- Illegal content, or content that violates someone's privacy.

Links that break these rules are blocked. Visitors to a blocked link see "This link has been blocked for violating our terms of service." instead of the destination, and accounts used for abuse can be suspended.

## Link safety checks

Every destination is checked when a link is created, when its destination changes, and when links come in through the [API](/docs/api) or a [CSV import](/docs/bulk-import). The checks include known-bad domains, suspicious URL patterns, and Google Safe Browsing. Live links are also re-checked against threat feeds several times a day.

When a destination fails a check, the link isn't created and you see one of these messages:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `This URL has been flagged as potentially unsafe and cannot be shortened. If you believe this is a mistake, please contact support.` | A threat database or our phishing detection flagged the URL. |
| `This URL belongs to a domain that has been blocked. If you believe this is a mistake, please contact support.` | The domain is on iShortn's blocklist. |
| `This URL looks suspicious and cannot be shortened. Please double-check the URL and try again, or contact support if you believe this is an error.` | The URL matches a pattern common in abuse, such as a raw IP address as the host. |

If you think a legitimate URL was flagged, email [support@ishortn.ink](mailto:support@ishortn.ink) with the URL.

## Report a harmful link

If a short link on `isht.app`, `isht.ink`, `ishortn.ink` or a custom domain led you somewhere harmful, report it on the [abuse report page](/abuse):

1. Paste the short link, such as `isht.app/abc123`.
2. Pick a category: phishing, malware, spam, impersonation, illegal content, privacy violation or other.
3. Add details and, optionally, your email so we can follow up.

Every report is reviewed, and links that break the rules are blocked. You can also email [support@ishortn.ink](mailto:support@ishortn.ink).
