iShortn turns long URLs into short links you can share anywhere, then shows you who clicked, from where and on what device. The same account also makes QR codes and link-in-bio pages, and you can bring your own domain.

## Create an account

[Sign up](/auth/sign-up) for an account. Every account starts on the Free plan, with no credit card needed. You can upgrade from [Settings → Billing](/dashboard/settings#billing) whenever you need more; see [Plans and limits](/docs/plans).

## Shorten your first link

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and click **New Link**.
2. Paste the long URL into **Destination URL**.
3. Optionally type a custom alias, such as `spring-sale`. Leave it empty and iShortn generates one for you.
4. Pick a domain. New links use `isht.app` unless you've chosen another default.
5. Click **Create Link**, then copy the short link and share it.

The link works straight away. Visitors who open `isht.app/spring-sale` are sent to your destination, and each visit is recorded in your analytics.

Every link can also have an expiry, a password, a scheduled start time, targeting rules and more. [Short links](/docs/short-links) covers each option and the plan it needs.

## See who clicked

Click a link in the dashboard to open its analytics: clicks over time, unique visitors, countries and cities, devices, browsers, operating systems and referrers. [Analytics](/docs/analytics) explains what's tracked and how long it's kept.
